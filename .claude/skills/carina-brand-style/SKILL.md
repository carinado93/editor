# Brand style

## Colours
Lilac #C9B3EC (text, pills, stickers) · light lilac bg #D6C6F0 (gradient (222,208,246)→(206,190,238)) · dark lilac #7A649E · plum #4A346E · butter yellow #FAE8A5 · cream #F4EEE4 · dark brown #4A3A30 · olive #75733B / #6B6F3A · gold #D6B46E (dark covers).
Her colour beats brand colours: a Strava-style card was asked "but with lilac". Text on lilac backgrounds = white with plum shadow or plum.

## Fonts (via @fontsource npm, woff2 → ttf with fontTools)
Titan One (bubble titles, pills) · Fredoka 600/700 (sub-lines, labels) · Inter 300/800 (UI cards, stats) · Anton (3D cylinder, torn labels) · Cinzel Decorative (magic) · Cormorant Garamond 600 italic (writes itself) · Noto Serif Display (covers) · Noto Color Emoji (size 109, embedded_color, then resize).
Titan has no "→" glyph: draw shapes or avoid.

## Layout
- Text never on face/hands; split words left/right of her body so no letter hides behind her head; ≥ ~200 px from frame edges; important text centred.
- Vertical safe zone (IG + YT Shorts): y 280–1250, x 90–900.
- Pop-in = back-ease scale (c=1.7) + alpha, words staggered 0.3–0.5 s; soft shadow (blur 6–8, plum, 45–75 %).
- Pills: lilac rounded pill, white Titan text, inner white pill with dark-lilac Fredoka (e.g. "× 30 min"), drawn stopwatch with rotating hand.

## Never
Colour grading of her footage · light-trail outline around her · dotted backgrounds · slow cursor-only intros · harsh boom/glitch/shake on calm prevention content unless asked · another creator's footage · emoji thumbs raining over everything (she removed "likes").

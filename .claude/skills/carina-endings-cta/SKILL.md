# Endings & CTAs (scripts in `scripts/`)

## Strava-style day stats (end.py) – chosen: "Share Activity phone"
- Freeze on her final gesture (kiss/wave). Background blurred + darkened (#16121E 75 %) + faint transparent checkerboard; her cut-out sharp in front (hand mask fixed!).
- Phone UI slides up on the right: "Close · Share Activity", checker card "TRANSPARENT" with stats (Work 11h 00m / Sports 2h 00m / Meditation 30m), lilac route line, italic wordmark with HER NAME (never the Strava logo), "Share to" row (lilac Story ring, Copy/Save/Link/More).
- Cursor taps Story → sticker flies out next to her, phone drops away. CTA pill "give kudos & follow for part 2". No thumbs rain (she removed it).
- Alternatives built: 6 stats counting behind her cut-out + route with emoji checkpoints; dark "Day in Work" recap card with ▲▼ joke comparisons.
- Day totals she gave: meditation 30m, sports 2h, editing 3h, writing 3h, marketing/client acquisition 2h30, telling Claude what to do 2h30 (= 11h work).

## Subscribe / like CTA (cta2.py)
- At the moment an object (coffee glass) fully covers the lens: lilac gradient fills from the centre as an iris (feathered circle, 0.3 s).
- On lilac: "any support in the form of" (Fredoka plum) / "a subscribe or like" (Titan white) / "would be highly appreciated 🤍"; buttons 👍 + lilac SUBSCRIBE; cursor clicks SUBSCRIBE → "🔔 SUBSCRIBED", then 👍 turns lilac.
- Iris closes after ~3.8 s and the clip continues. Original audio paused, soft pops/clicks.
- She rejected the first version where the blurred coffee was still visible behind the text.

## Thank-you heart (ty2.py)
- "THANK" left / "YOU" right drop in BEHIND her before the air kiss.
- On the kiss: lilac heart with white glow grows from her mouth (ease-in, 15 frames) to cover the frame; end on pure lilac (she cut the rest: ends at full lilac, 5.5 s).
- Optional end card: heart-shaped window with her frozen face on lilac + floating mini hearts + "and yes, it was cringe for me to ask for that 🙈 / so… thank you 💜".
- v1 (plain text + heart sticker) was "not matching the editing before" – always use the cut-out/behind-text level of polish.

## Rules
- YouTube Shorts must be vertical: landscape edits → 1080x1920 with black bars (`scale=1080:-2,pad=1080:1920:0:(oh-ih)/2:black`). Landscape files post as normal videos even with type "short". Up to 3 min is fine.
- Media: Metricool takes Google-Drive file links (her Drive is linked). Upload files to her Drive folder `Selbständigkeit/social media content/fuer claude` first.
- To stop a scheduled post: update it with `draft: true` (full original content). To swap the video: update media + time.
- YouTube data: title, type short/video, public, madeForKids false, category PEOPLE_BLOGS, 5 tags. Custom thumbnails only work on verified channels – otherwise she uploads the 16:9 thumbnail in YouTube Studio.

## Captions
Her voice, first person, honest, a little self-ironic, prevention only (no healing promises, no diagnosis). Structure: hook line → what the video shows → her honest reflection → question to comment. Exactly 5 hashtags.

DE example set: #tagausmeinemleben #selbstständig #homeoffice #selbstständigkeit #frauenimbusiness

## Pinned comment (YouTube)
Turn the video's interactive question into a fill-in comment (e.g. rate the 6 burnout areas 1–5, "reply with your lowest + one small change"), add "I read every comment 💜" and a short not-a-diagnosis line for health topics.
